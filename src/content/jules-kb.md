# Jules Knowledge Base — RecoverMérida

## Hospitals in Mérida

Mérida has 4 JCI-accredited hospitals for international patients:

- **Star Médica Mérida** — JCI Accredited, Canadian Healthcare Council Level 3. Ranked #1 Southern Region Mexico (Expansión, 2025). Address: Calle 26 199, Altabrisa. Phone: +52 999 930-2880.
- **Faro del Mayab (CHRISTUS Muguerza)** — JCI Accredited, Mayo Clinic Strategic Alliance. Address: Calle 24 S/N, Temozón Norte. Phone: +52 999 689-4500.
- **Clínica de Mérida** — Mercy Hospital Miami Affiliate. English-speaking staff. Address: Av. Itzáes 242, García Ginerés. Phone: +52 999 942-1800.
- **Centro Médico de Las Américas** — Mercy Hospital Miami Associate. Maternity + specialist center. Address: Calle 54 365, near Paseo de Montejo. Phone: +52 999 926-2111.

All hospitals have bilingual staff and international patient coordinators.

## Safety

- Mérida has been ranked the **safest city in Mexico** for over a decade by INEGI (Mexico's national statistics institute).
- Crime rates are comparable to mid-size Canadian cities.
- Permanent expat community of **10,000+ North Americans**.
- Consistently listed among the safest cities in Latin America.

## RecoverMérida Services

- Hospital and surgeon coordination
- Recovery-certified housing — two properties in northern Mérida (Altabrisa and Temozón Norte neighborhoods), within 15 min of all 4 hospitals
- 24/7 AI WhatsApp monitoring that tracks symptoms and alerts nursing staff
- Bilingual coordination between patient and surgical team
- Daily wellness check-ins
- Meal preparation and medication management
- Physical therapy coordination
- 30-day remote monitoring after flying home

No dedicated recovery hotels exist in Mérida — RecoverMérida is the only certified recovery infrastructure.
